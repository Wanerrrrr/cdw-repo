12 / THE CHALLENGE

REVEAL HIDDEN LOGIC WITHOUT INVENTING A FALSELY COMPLETE MODEL.

RESEARCH

Distinguish fixed cycles, actuated buttons, APS devices, and real malfunctions.

DESIGN

Represent uncertainty without blaming pedestrians or overwhelming users.

TECHNICAL

Connect field data, a signal state machine, timelines, and a physical prototype.

SCOPE

Begin with one intersection and expand only after the observation method works.
