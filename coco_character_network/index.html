HOW TO READ IT

Family holds the network together.

The Rivera family forms the dense center of the graph. Music creates both bridges and conflict, while memory links the living and the dead. Hover over a character to isolate their immediate relationships.

DATA METHOD

Film relationships translated into two CSV files.

nodes.csv stores character attributes and edges.csv stores pairwise relationships. The visualization loads both external files with D3, calculates each character's degree, and uses it to scale the skull icons.
