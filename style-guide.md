# Aesthetic

## Primary Style

**Aliencore**

Aliencore explores themes of:

- extraterrestrial environments
- mysterious technology
- space exploration
- digital archives
- speculative futures
- advanced civilizations

Instead of emphasizing horror, the interface focuses on curiosity, discovery, and scientific observation.

# Typography

## Rubik Gemstones

Used for:

- Project Title

Purpose:

Creates a crystalline, futuristic appearance that immediately establishes the Aliencore identity.

---

## Rubik Moonrocks

Used for:

- Section Titles

Purpose:

Provides playful geometric forms while remaining readable.

---

## IBM Plex Mono

Used for:

- Header
- Metadata
- Archive labels
- Technical interface elements

Purpose:

References command-line interfaces and research databases.

---

## Inter

Used for:

- Paragraphs
- Tables
- General content

Purpose:

Maintains readability for long-form academic content.

# Motion

The interface uses subtle animation.

Current effects include:

- glowing text
- animated stars
- hover transitions
- neon lighting

Animations are intentionally restrained to preserve readability and avoid distracting from the academic content.

---


# Accessibility

Design choices were made to improve readability.

- high contrast between text and background
- consistent spacing
- limited color palette
- clear visual hierarchy
- readable body typography

# Reflection

The website explores how interface design can influence the interpretation of information.

By combining academic research with a speculative Aliencore visual language, the project transforms a precedent study into an immersive archival experience rather than a conventional webpage.
